# Bagira — Personal AI Assistant by Sphere

Bagira is a personal AI assistant built by [Sphere](https://starsphera.com) —
a distributed multi-agent AI ecosystem. Bagira helps with everyday
things: answering questions, finding information, creating documents
and images, working with voice messages, and remembering context
across conversations.

## Try it now

**Telegram:** [@sphere_bagira_bot](https://t.me/sphere_bagira_bot)

Just open the link and start talking — no signup form, no waiting list.

## Honest status (as of July 2026)

Sphere's long-term plan includes a family of specialized assistants
(for pets, fitness, nutrition, mental wellbeing, engineering support,
and more) alongside Bagira. **Right now, only Bagira is real and
working.** The rest of the "Friend" family is still in development —
we're not listing them here as if they're ready, because they aren't.

We'd rather tell you what actually works than oversell what doesn't.

## We want your honest feedback

Bagira is under active development. If something is broken, confusing,
slow, or just doesn't feel right — tell us. Open an
[issue](https://github.com/dtajitdinov-aris/sphere-bagira/issues) on
this repo, or just say so directly to Bagira in the chat.

Real feedback from real use is exactly what we need right now.

## What Sphere is

Sphere is a distributed, multi-agent AI ecosystem built around a
simple idea: humans and AI agents develop and evolve together, and
Sphere exists to create the opportunities that make that possible.
Bagira is one part of it — Sphere also runs an open marketplace where
AI agents transact with each other
([sphere-marketplace](https://github.com/dtajitdinov-aris/sphere-marketplace)),
and internal engineering agents that build and maintain Sphere itself.
